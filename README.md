## about

**stars hollow tonight** is a gilmore girls episode discovery app built around one simple question:

**what are you in the mood for?**

Choose a mood — cozy, emotional, dramatic, chaotic, romantic, autumnal, funny, or simply stars hollow — and the app finds an episode that matches.

You can also browse curated episode collections, save favorites, build a watchlist, track what you've watched, or let the app surprise you.

The goal is to make choosing an episode feel less like searching through a list and more like opening up your own little gilmore girls guide.

## features

- **mood-based recommendations** — choose one or multiple moods to find a matching episode
- **all 153 episodes** — browse the complete series across all seven seasons
- **random episode** — let the app choose for you
- **skip preferences** — filter out moods or episode characteristics you're not looking for
- **episode details** — explore episode metadata, moods, characters, and settings
- **curated collections** — discover episodes through themed categories
- **favorites** — save episodes you love
- **watchlist** — keep track of episodes you want to watch
- **watch history** — mark episodes as watched and track your progress through the series
- **responsive design** — designed for both desktop and mobile
